Original geometry author: OPEN BREACH
License: CC0 1.0 Universal, as recorded in Original-Assets.txt.
Includes four fitted plate carriers (Veil V1, Frame V2, Swift V3, Bastion V4),
Dot/Scope/Suppressor/Extended Magazine attachments, Probe C1 observation
camera, Flare F1 flashbang, Haze S1 smoke canister, Knock B1 entry tool,
and original identification patch geometry. No real manufacturer markings,
borrowed game icons, textures, sound recordings or trademark logos included.
