Original special operations models

The original procedural Blender models generated by scripts/build_historical_assets.py
were authored for this CQB project. They contain no downloaded commercial meshes,
trademarks, emblems, airline branding, or insignia. They may be used, modified, and
distributed under the project's existing original-asset CC0 1.0 Universal terms.
To the extent possible under law, these project-authored reconstruction meshes
are dedicated to the public domain. The full text is in CC0-1.0.txt.

Generic published dimension references are not an assertion that these readable
game reconstructions reproduce surveyed engineering drawings. Reconstructed
interiors, decorative details and unmeasured buildings are identified in
licenses/provenance/SPECIAL_OPS_ASSET_PROVENANCE.json.

The six adapted Kenney Nature Kit assets retain their separate CC0-1.0 license:
licenses/special-ops-assets-kenney-nature.txt. Their source archive and original GLB
files are preserved under assets/source/historical/.
